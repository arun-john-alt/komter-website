Built in Chennai, German drivetrains

  1. Airends ground on German KAPP machines

    E Series airends are designed and processed on German KAPP grinding machines.

  2. High Pressure: German airend technology

    The 2-stage High Pressure range, 50 to 150 HP and 25 to 40 bar, uses German airend technology.

  3. Portables with a German-designed airend

    Diesel portables carry a bigger, German-designed screw airend. The KDM 450 T delivers 450 cfm at 14 bar.

HE Series: oil keeps the airend at about 80 °C regardless of load.

Other named parts

  • Bearings

    SKF heavy-duty bearings on High Pressure units.

  • Controls

    Siemens / Schneider relays and contactors in the Premium control box.

  • Drive

    Direct drive on E Series and portables.
